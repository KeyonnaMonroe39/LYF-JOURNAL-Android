# LYF4Kids Visual Design System

This project follows the LYF4Kids warm SEL design direction:

- LYF Deep Plum `#3E2437`
- Healing Mauve `#8E5A6F`
- Rose Warmth `#D9899E`
- Champagne Gold `#F4D7A1`
- Soft Cream `#FFF7EC`
- Charcoal Ink `#1F1F24`
- Feelings Sky `#72C7E7`
- Growth Green `#7BC47F`
- Courage Coral `#FF7A6B`
- Joy Yellow `#F6C85F`
- Imagination Lavender `#BFA7F2`
- Kindness Peach `#FFC6A8`
- Ocean Teal `#2F7D8C`

Typography:

- Headings: Poppins
- Body: Nunito Sans
- Accent: Fraunces

Experience principles:

- Cream backgrounds, rounded cards, soft shadows, emotional color coding.
- Warm and illustration-ready, not clinical or generic-stock heavy.
- Accessible focus states, large tap targets, reduced-motion support.
